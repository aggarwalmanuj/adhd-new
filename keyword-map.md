## ⚠️ Important methodology note (read first)

Your CSV is a **Keyword Tool / Google Ads–style export**, not a KWFinder export. It has **no real SEO Keyword Difficulty (KD) column**. It has a **Competition** column, but that is the **Google Ads auction metric (0–100)** — advertiser demand, *not* organic ranking difficulty.

Per your instruction, this report shows **both**:
- **Competition (Ads 0–100)** — straight from your data.
- **Est. SEO KD (0–100)** — my estimate, based on SERP type (listicles/stats/questions = easier) and how niche the term is. **These are directional estimates, not measured KD.** For verified KD, pull these terms through Ahrefs/Semrush/KWFinder.
